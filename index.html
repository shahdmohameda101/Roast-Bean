OUR HERITAGE

Made for Coffee Lovers

Founded with a deep reverence for the bean, Roast & Bean began as an unassuming drum roaster tucked behind an 1890s brick mill. Today, every roast profile is personally calibrated by our master roasters to draw out subtle, naturally developed notes of cacao, wild plum, and golden honey.

We partner exclusively with regenerative family estates, roasting in hyper-fresh small batches. Our cafe sanctuaries are purposely slow spaces—where oak timber, soft amber light, and the gentle hiss of espresso steam welcome you to unwind.
